Chapter II: Gemological Master Criteria (The 4 C's)

Universal grading standards formulated by the Gemological Institute of America (GIA).

1. Carat (Mass & Weight Cliffs)

One metric carat equals 200 milligrams (0.200 grams). Diamonds and colored gemstones do not appreciate linearly with weight; they scale exponentially.

Specific Gravities:
• Diamond:3.52 g/cm³
• Sapphire (Corundum):4.00 g/cm³ (heavier)
• Alexandrite (Chrysoberyl):3.73 g/cm³

2. Cut & Proportion Physics

Cut defines how light refracts internally rather than leaking through the pavilion (extinction).

  • Table Percentage (54-62%): Ratio of the top flat facet to overall stone diameter.
  • Depth Percentage (60-62.5%): Ideal range for total internal reflection in brilliant cuts.
  • Shapes: Round Brilliant (highest fire), Step Emerald (parallel hall-of-mirrors), Cushion (soft pillow corners), Oval, and Pear.

3. Color Tiers (D through J + Fancy)

White diamonds are evaluated by absence of body color. Fancy colored diamonds (Pinks, Canaries) and Corundum reverse this: vivid saturation commands astronomical premiums.

4. Clarity Scale (Internal Inclusions)

Examined at 10× optical magnification. Internal imperfections diminish light travel and structural integrity.

FL / IF: Flawless. Zero inclusions visible under 10×.
VVS1 - VVS2: Extremely minute pinpoints; difficult for master graders to find.
VS1 - VS2: Minor crystals or feathers off-table; eye-clean to clients.
SI1 - SI2: Noticeable inclusions under 10×; visible to trained naked eye.
I1: Prominent cleavage fissures that jeopardize stone durability.

Chapter III: Optical Phenomena & Micro-Inclusions

Differentiating unheated natural gemstones from treated goods using the bench's spectral light switches.

Natural Alexandrite: The Color-Change Miracle Pleochroic Shift

Chromium ($Cr^{3+}$) ions in the chrysoberyl lattice have balanced transmission windows in green and red wavelengths.

5500K Daylight: Rich in blue-green wavelengths. The stone transmits peacock teal-green.
2700K Incandescent Lamp: Rich in warm red wavelengths. The stone shifts to vibrant raspberry-rhodolite red.
Unheated Ceylon Sapphires: Rutile Silk Needles Darkfield Diagnostics

Natural untreated corundum crystallizes with fine microscopic needles of titanium dioxide (Rutile, $TiO_2$) that intersect at exact 60° angles. Under 10× darkfield illumination, unbroken needles prove the gem was never heat-treated, immediately increasing wholesale market value by 60%!

Types of Internal Flaws Under the Loupe
Pinpoints (White dots) Microscopic mineral crystals or gas bubbles suspended in the matrix.
Feathers (White lines) Internal stress cracks or cleavage lines. Risky if they reach the girdle.
Crystals (Dark squares) Foreign mineral deposits (e.g. garnet or diopside) trapped during formation.
